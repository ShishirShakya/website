# Website (Shishir Shakya)

Personal academic site: CV, publications lists, projects, and paper-facing explainers built with MyST.

## Language

**Publication**:
A bibliographic record of a research output, maintained in the CV publications list (`book/cv/publications.md` and generated themed pages).
_Avoid_: Paper note, blog post, explainer

**Paper note**:
A short, policy-facing explainer page tied to one Publication; paraphrases findings for non-specialist readers and links to the canonical PDF or DOI.
_Avoid_: Blog post, full paper, abstract dump, publication list entry

**Facts block**:
The fixed, human-verified claim fields on a Paper note (policy hook, main finding, data and setting, plain-language design, one caveat, PDF or DOI) that substantive narrative may rely on.
_Avoid_: Abstract paste, unverified AI summary

**Stub**:
A Paper note with citation and link only, marked **Status**: stub in the page body, whose Facts block is not yet verified against the PDF.
_Avoid_: Verified note, published claim
